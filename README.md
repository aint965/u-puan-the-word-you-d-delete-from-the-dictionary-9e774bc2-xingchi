## The entry

If one word has to be erased from the dictionary forever, it is **moist** — the famously most-hated word in the English language. Nobody ever asked for it, nobody enjoys saying it, and yet it shows up everywhere, from cake descriptions to weather reports, squelching along with its damp, clammy sound. Delete it and the language loses nothing: cakes can be "tender", soil can be "damp", and nobody will ever again wince at a perfectly innocent sentence. The joke needs no explanation — the moment the word is gone, everyone feels the relief.

## How it works

The program writes exactly `moist` to `/output/entry.txt` — five bytes, no trailing newline, nothing else.
